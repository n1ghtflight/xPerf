About

XPerf plans performance, route, cruise level and fuel for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024. For flight simulation only — not for real-world navigation or flight planning.

  • Airports and navaids: OurAirports (public domain)
  • US airways, procedures, preferred routes and charts: FAA NASR and d-TPP (public domain)
  • GA, turboprop and business-jet performance: XPerf's own table from manufacturer, POH and magazine figures (sources listed per aircraft)
  • Airliner performance: OpenAP by TU Delft
  • Winds aloft and terrain: Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0)
  • METAR and TAF: aviationweather.gov (NOAA / National Weather Service)
  • Maps: © OpenStreetMap contributors, OpenTopoMap (CC BY-SA); map library Leaflet (BSD-2-Clause)
  • Flight plans you import: SimBrief, fetched only when you ask

Found a bug, or a number that looks wrong? Report it on GitHub — include the aircraft, the airports and what you expected.

XPerf is a free hobby project, not affiliated with Microsoft, Asobo Studio, Navigraph or any data provider.
